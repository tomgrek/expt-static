Job history is part of Pro

Your simulators, your machine control and your local saves all keep working exactly as they do now — nothing here is being taken away.

What Pro adds is memory. Every job gets archived as it runs: how long it took, how far it got, the feed and spindle trace, the line an alarm stopped it on, and the material and power it was cut at. Then it is all readable from this page, from inside the apps, and by your own AI agent — so “what did I cut that walnut at in March” has an answer.
